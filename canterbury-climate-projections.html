About these projections

Historical values represent average annual conditions for 1995–2014. Future periods are 2041–2060 and 2081–2100. The app uses SSP2-4.5 and SSP3-7.0.

The 10th and 90th percentiles are interpolated across six individual downscaled global climate models. They show the spread among those models and should not be interpreted as probability bounds or confidence intervals.

Most temperature, rainfall and derived indicators are based on bias-corrected projections. Relative humidity, solar radiation and wind variables are spatially regridded to the ~5 km grid and are not bias-corrected in the source dataset.

These results are intended for climate information and screening. Conditions can vary within an area, especially with elevation, distance from the coast and local topography.

Data and acknowledgements

Data are from the New Zealand CMIP6 climate projections produced by NIWA and distributed by the Ministry for the Environment. Six CMIP6 global models were dynamically downscaled to a national grid of approximately 5 km.

Gibson, P. B., Stuart, S., Sood, A., Stone, D., Rampal, N., Lewis, H., Broadbent, A., Thatcher, M., & Morgenstern, O. (2024). Dynamical downscaling CMIP6 models over New Zealand: added value of climatology and extremes. Climate Dynamics. Dataset methodology publication
