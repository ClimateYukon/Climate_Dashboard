About these projections

Historical values represent average conditions for 1971–2000. Future values show projected changes from that historical period.

These results are intended for climate information and screening. Conditions can vary considerably within a large territory, especially with changes in elevation and geography.

Data and acknowledgements

Climate projection data are from the Canadian Downscaled Climate Scenarios Multivariate dataset for CMIP6, or CanDCS-M6.
